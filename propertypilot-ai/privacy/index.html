PropertyPilot AI

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 6, 2026

PropertyPilot AI is operated by Ortiz Venture Holdings LLC. This policy explains how PropertyPilot AI handles information in the iOS app.

Information We Collect

PropertyPilot AI processes property and unit details, lease information, tenant and owner contact information, maintenance requests and attached photos or video, inspection records, subscription status, device diagnostics needed to operate the app, and support messages sent by the user.

Payments

Rent and subscription payments are processed by Apple and our third-party payment partners. PropertyPilot AI does not store full payment card or bank account numbers.

Accounting Integrations

If a user connects QuickBooks Online or Xero, PropertyPilot AI exchanges only the accounting data needed to keep both systems in sync, using each provider's official API and authorization flow. Users can disconnect an integration at any time in Settings.

Subscriptions

Purchases are processed by Apple through In-App Purchase and may be synchronized through RevenueCat for entitlement management. Payment information is handled by Apple and is not collected by PropertyPilot AI.

Notifications

Notifications are optional and are used for rent reminders, maintenance updates, and app updates. Users can manage notification permissions in iOS Settings.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data may be shared with service providers only as needed to operate subscriptions, payments, accounting sync, support, security, and app functionality.

Not Legal, Tax, or Accounting Advice

PropertyPilot AI is property management software only. It does not provide legal, tax, or accounting advice, and it is not a property manager, broker, or law firm.
