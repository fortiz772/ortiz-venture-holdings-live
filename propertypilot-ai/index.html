Core Features

Everything a small portfolio needs, nothing it doesn't.

💳 Online Rent Collection

Tenants pay by ACH or card. Funds post automatically and every payment is logged for owners in real time.

📋 Offline-First Inspections

Walkthrough photos and notes save to the device instantly and sync once you're back online — no freezing, no redone walkthroughs.

🔧 Maintenance Tracking

Tenants submit requests with photos and video, vendors get routed automatically, and updates go out without a chased email thread.

🏠 Tenant & Owner Portals

Separate dashboards: tenants see balances and requests, owners see statements and performance — nobody wades through the other person's screen.

✍️ E-Sign Leases

Send, sign, and store leases and renewals without printing, scanning, or a separate e-signature subscription.

A genuine two-way sync with QuickBooks Online and Xero, with a plain-language page showing exactly what syncs and what doesn't.
