Subscription Terms

Effective date: August 6, 2026

PropertyPilot AI is an auto-renewing monthly subscription offered through Apple In-App Purchase.

Plan

PropertyPilot AI includes a 7-day free trial. After the free trial, the subscription is $4.99 USD per month for up to 10 units, plus $0.75 USD per additional unit per month, unless canceled. Pricing shown in the app may vary by region and is provided by Apple's StoreKit product metadata where available.

Billing

Payment is charged to the user's Apple ID. The subscription renews automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current billing period.

Managing or Canceling

Users can manage or cancel subscriptions through Apple account settings. Restore Purchases is available inside the PropertyPilot AI app.

Refunds

Refund requests can be submitted in-app or to cisco@ortizventureholdings.com; we aim to resolve every billing dispute within 24 hours. Refunds for Apple In-App Purchase charges are ultimately issued by Apple.
